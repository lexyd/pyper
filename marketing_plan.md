# Marketing Plan: Pyper (Comprehensive Edition)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Core Narrative**: Pyper is the "CFO in your pocket" for the Creator Economy. We don't just "manage deals"—we **recover lost revenue** caused by administrative chaos.

## 1. Executive Summary
Pyper solves the "SaaS Sprawl" and "Inbox Chaos" that plagues professional creators. By consolidating CRM, Contract Analysis, and Deliverables into one "Revenue Operating System," we help creators look like agencies and operate like businesses.

## 2. Target Audience Segmentation & ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

We are not targeting *all* creators. We are targeting **Professional Creators** who are already making money but losing time.

### Avatar A: The "Overwhelmed Solo"
*   **Profile**: 50k-500k followers. Full-time or transitioning to full-time. No manager.
*   **Pain Point**: Sends invoices late. Forgets to follow up on "ghosted" brand emails. Signs contracts without reading them.
*   **Trigger Event**: Missed a $5k payment because an invoice fell through the cracks.
*   **Pyper Pitch**: "The Assistant you can't afford to hire yet."

### Avatar B: The "Small Creative Team"
*   **Profile**: 500k+ followers. Has a video editor or an assistant. Maybe a spouse helping out.
*   **Pain Point**: "Did you reply to Coke?" "No, I thought you did." Version control hell.
*   **Trigger Event**: Brand complained about a wrong deliverable being posted.
*   **Pyper Pitch**: "The central brain for your creative business."

## 3. The "Revenue Recovery" Funnel (Acquisition Strategy)

Moving beyond generic "brand awareness" to specific, high-intent acquisition channels.

### Channel 1: High-Utility Content Marketing (SEO & Social)
*   **Objective**: Capture search intent for "boring" but critical business problems.
*   **Primary Keywords**: *Influencer specific invoice template*, *Brand deal contract red flags*, *How to chase late payments from brands*.
*   **Content Assets**:
    *   **"The Hall of Shame" (Short-form Video)**: Dissecting anonymous bad contracts. (e.g., "This clause allows them to use your face on a billboard forever for $500.")
    *   **"The Lost Money Calculator"**: Interactive web tool. Inputs: Monthly Inquiries, Deal Size. Output: "You are burning $12,000/year in missed follow-ups."

### Channel 2: Viral Product Loops (Product-Led Growth)
*   **The "Public Media Kit"**: Every Pyper user gets a public `pyper.co/username` page.
    *   **Why it spreads**: Creators send this link to brands. Brands see "Powered by Pyper" when they pay or review content.
*   **"Refer-a-Creator" Logic**:
    *   **Incentive**: Unlock "AI Legal Review" for 3 months by referring 1 friend.
    *   **Why it works**: Creators talk. They are in group chats complaining about brands. Give them a solution to share.

### Channel 3: Strategic Partnerships
*   **Talent Managers**: Managers hate chasing their talent for deliverables. Pyper makes their talent more organized.
    *   *Pitch*: "Get your roster on Pyper, and spend 50% less time nagging them about deadlines."
*   **Utility Tools**: Integration partnerships with tools like **Tally** (Forms) or **Notion** (Knowledge base).

## 4. Lifecycle Marketing (Retention & Activation)

How do we turn a sign-up into a power user?

### The "First 7 Days" Email Drip
1.  **Day 0 (Welcome)**: "Stop the bleeding. Set up your first 'Deal Pipeline' in 3 minutes."
2.  **Day 1 (Value)**: "The 3 contract clauses that kill your earnings." (Educate first, sell tool second).
3.  **Day 3 (Feature Highlight)**: "How to send a 'Magic Review Link' to a brand so they approve faster."
4.  **Day 7 (Social Proof)**: Case study: "How Sarah recovered $4k in lost invoices using Pyper."

### "Churn Prevention" Triggers
*   **Inactivity**: If a user hasn't logged a deal in 14 days -> Email: "Slow month? Here's a template to re-engage past brand partners."
*   **Feature Gaps**: If they use the CRM but not the Contract Reader -> In-app tooltip: "Did you know you can upload that PDF here?"

## 5. 90-Day Go-To-Market Roadmap

### Phase 1: The "Hype & Fear" (Month 1)
*   **Goal**: Waitlist growth to 1,000 creators.
*   **Tactic**: Heavy social content focusing on "Horror Stories" (Unpaid invoices, bad contracts). Position Pyper as the shield and sword.
*   **Deliverable**: Launch "Bad Contract Scanner" (MVP feature or manual concierge).

### Phase 2: The "Alpha Cohort" (Month 2)
*   **Goal**: 50 Power Users providing feedback.
*   **Tactic**: Manual onboarding. "Do things that don't scale." We set up their workspace for them.
*   **Metric**: "Weekly Active Deals" (Are they actually managing real money?)

### Phase 3: The "Public Beta" (Month 3)
*   **Goal**: Open access, viral loops active.
*   **Tactic**: Enable the public `pyper.co` profiles. Encourage users to put it in their bio ("Book me on Pyper").
*   **Launch Event**: "Revenue Recovery Week" – Webinar/Workshop series.

## 6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target (Launch) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Waitlist Velocity** | New signups per week | > 100/week |
| **Activation Rate** | % of users who create >1 Deal Card | > 40% |
| **Viral Coefficient** | Average invites sent per user | > 1.2 |
| **"Revenue Managed"** | Total $ value of deals in Pipelines | > $100k (Month 1) |
