# Project Reference: Deal Flow & Pain Points

## Creator-Brand Deal Flow

1. **Initial Outreach**
   - Brand/agency sends collaboration email
   - Creator reviews offer (fit with audience, compensation, deliverables)
   - Creator responds with interest or counter-proposal

2. **Negotiation & Terms**
   - Back-and-forth on rates, deliverables, timeline
   - Discuss creative freedom, usage rights, exclusivity
   - Clarify: number of posts, format (Reel, story, video), talking points
   - Negotiate payment terms (upfront, upon delivery, net 30/60)

3. **Contract/Agreement**
   - Brand sends contract or creator sends their standard agreement
   - Review legal terms, usage rights, FTC disclosure requirements
   - Both parties sign (often via DocuSign or similar)

4. **Content Creation & Approval**
   - Creator develops content concept
   - Draft review - Send draft/storyboard to brand for approval (common for bigger deals)
   - Brand provides feedback/revisions
   - Creator makes adjustments
   - Final approval from brand

5. **Publishing**
   - Creator posts content on agreed date/time
   - Includes proper FTC disclosures (#ad, #sponsored)
   - May need to use specific brand tags, links, or codes

6. **Deliverables & Reporting**
   - Send brand screenshots/links to live content
   - Provide analytics (views, engagement, clicks, conversions)
   - May need to keep content live for agreed duration
   - Some deals require ongoing story mentions or updates

7. **Invoicing & Payment**
   - Creator sends invoice (if not paid upfront)
   - Brand processes through their system
   - Payment arrives (can take 30-90 days depending on terms)
   - Creator follows up on late payments if needed

8. **Relationship Management**
   - Thank you note/follow-up
   - Add contact to CRM for future opportunities
   - Track if they want to work together again

## Common Pain Points Your Tool Could Address
- Tracking multiple deals at different stages simultaneously
- Following up on contracts not returned or payments overdue
- Remembering deliverable details (posting dates, approval deadlines)
- Finding old conversations buried in email threads
- Analytics reporting - gathering and sending performance data
- Payment tracking - knowing who owes what and when
